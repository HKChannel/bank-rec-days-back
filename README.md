# Bank Rec Days-Back Calculator

A single-page app in one file (`index.html`) with no dependencies. It's the companion tool for an edition of the LinkedIn newsletter **AI in Finance, Done Right**: **"A $128M Nonprofit Cut Its Bank Reconciliations in Half by Writing AI Matching Rules in Plain English"**.

## Features
- **Tab 1, Days back:** Enter your bank transactions a month, minutes per manual match today and per exception with AI, the number of bank and clearing accounts, review and sign-off hours per account, staff and productive hours, and staff cost. Pick an auto-match rate or use a preset (CAFB's 95%, a cautious 85%, or 70% for messy feeds). See business days today and with AI matching, days back a month and the value a year. A warning shows when exceptions would take as long as matching by hand does today.
- **Tab 2, Control-first readiness:** Six steps to put in place before turning on AI matching, from picking one rules-heavy reconciliation to signing off every exception. You get a readiness dial, the biggest gaps and a plan you can copy.

The demo figures are made up. CAFB results come from a vendor-published case study. Everything runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded.
